## Attribution limits

No quoted inaccessible standard or book text was used. No citation is evidence that Toque executed a method. Public page identity supports credit, but origins of broad practices are not necessarily unique: Sato credits Kerievsky; BDD/ATDD overlap; Toque canary, epistemic taxonomy, thresholds and schemas are explicitly local designs. Historical inspiration remains credited here after removal from active claims. No effectiveness result was inferred from an impressive organization name.

Unlinked attributions were also checked as claims: the baseline's Replay.build “67%” statistic, its unspecified GitHub issue #24256 role-decay assertion, and its secondary IFScale interpretation lacked sufficient primary support for the asserted generalization. They were withdrawn, not repaired with invented bibliography. The “100x” cost-of-change assertion similarly lacked an appropriate cited empirical basis for this workflow. These are not additional resolving URLs and do not inflate the URL counts above.

The final reopen covered all **29** unique external URLs after the last source addition; all returned readable source content. The official hook reference also explicitly documents `command` plus `args` exec form, matching Toque's declared registry shape. That verifies the configuration's documented host contract, not a live hook invocation.
